Overview

Spain’s Digital Nomad Visa targets non-EU remote workers employed by, or freelancing for, companies outside Spain, with an income threshold of roughly 200% of Spain’s minimum wage, about €2,442 per month in 2026. It is valid for up to 2 years and renewable. For those with a confirmed Spanish employer, standard work-permit and highly qualified professional routes remain available, each with its own path to longer-term residence.

Questions about moving to Spain

Can I work for a Spanish client on the Digital Nomad visa?+

The route is built around income from clients or an employer outside Spain; limited Spanish-sourced income is allowed under specific conditions we confirm case by case.

Does this visa lead to permanent residence?+

It can, through renewal and continued residence over time, following Spain’s standard long-term residence rules.
