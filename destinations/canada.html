Destination Guide

Canada: our leading focus, with sixteen ways in

Canada runs a dual system: federal Express Entry, selected through category-based draws for healthcare, French language, trades and transport, alongside provincial nominee programs matched to a specific region. Between the two, most serious applicants have more than one live route.

Overview

We built our services hub specifically for Canada because it is where our team, including our President, has the deepest direct experience, including prior work inside Immigration Canada itself. If you already know your route, go directly to Express Entry, Provincial Nominee Programs, the Healthcare Pathway, business work permits, or study permits. If you are not sure, the fastest path is a paid consultation, where we compare every option against your specific profile in one session.
