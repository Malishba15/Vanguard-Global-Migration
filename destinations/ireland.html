Ireland: Critical Skills and General Employment Permits

English-speaking, EU-adjacent, and built around two clear employer-sponsored permit types, with salary thresholds set to rise gradually through the rest of the decade.

Overview

Ireland runs two main employer-sponsored routes. The Critical Skills Employment Permit covers occupations on the highly-skilled, in-demand list, with a minimum salary lowered to €40,904 from March 1, 2026. The General Employment Permit covers a broader range of roles at a €36,605 minimum, with further gradual increases planned through 2030. Critical Skills permit holders generally reach family reunification and long-term residence faster than General Employment Permit holders.

Frequently Asked

Questions about moving to Ireland

Which permit is faster to permanent status?+

Critical Skills, since it is built for occupations Ireland specifically wants to retain long-term.

Can my family join me sooner under Critical Skills?+

Generally yes, family reunification timelines are more favourable under this permit than the General Employment Permit.
