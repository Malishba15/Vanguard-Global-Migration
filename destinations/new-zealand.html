Overview

The Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) requires a confirmed job offer from an employer accredited by Immigration New Zealand, generally an applicant age of 55 or under, and a wage meeting the current threshold, around NZD $35 per hour in early 2026. From there, the Skilled Migrant Category points system is the main route to residence, weighing your age, qualifications, and New Zealand work experience once you have built up time in the country.

Questions about moving to New Zealand

Is there an age limit for New Zealand work visas?+

The Accredited Employer Work Visa generally applies to applicants 55 or under; we confirm current settings at your consultation.

Does the AEWV lead automatically to residence?+

Not automatically, but qualifying work experience under it typically builds toward the points-tested Skilled Migrant Category.
