Overview

Finland’s Specialist Residence Permit bypasses the standard labour market test and is processed on a fast track of roughly two weeks for qualifying applicants. It requires a confirmed job offer, a minimum salary of about €3,937 per month in 2026, and a relevant higher-education degree or equivalent expertise. It is granted for up to 2 years, and a spouse can generally work in Finland without needing a separate work authorization.

Frequently Asked

Questions about moving to Finland

Do I need a labour market test for this permit?+

No, that is the specific advantage of the Specialist Residence Permit over Finland’s standard work-permit route.

Can my spouse work in Finland too?+

Generally yes, without needing a separate work permit, once you hold a valid Specialist Residence Permit.
