Overview

The Skills in Demand visa replaced the former Temporary Skill Shortage visa as Australia’s main employer-sponsored route, requiring a salary generally above AUD 70,000 and an occupation on the current skilled occupation list. Permanent outcomes typically run through state or territory nomination, which adds points and prioritizes applicants in occupations a specific state needs, or through the points-tested Skilled Independent visa for candidates who don’t need sponsorship at all.

Frequently Asked

Questions about moving to Australia

Do I need a state nomination to move to Australia?+

Not always, some applicants qualify for the points-tested Skilled Independent visa without one, but nomination often improves your odds and timeline.

Is a job offer required?+

For the employer-sponsored Skills in Demand route, yes. For points-tested routes, not necessarily.
