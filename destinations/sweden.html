Sweden: employer-sponsored work permits under the 2026 wage-floor reform

Overview

Effective June 1, 2026, Sweden raised its standard work-permit salary floor to 90% of the national median wage, roughly SEK 34,470 per month. A list of 152 shortage occupations, including healthcare and IT roles, qualifies for a lower 75% floor, about SEK 28,725. Applications are employer-initiated through Migrationsverket, Sweden’s migration agency, with a fast-track of roughly 30 days available for qualifying highly-skilled roles, and standard processing closer to 4 months.
