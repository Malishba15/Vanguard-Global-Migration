Frequently asked questions

Are you licensed to provide immigration advice?

Yes. Your file is reviewed under RCIC R416527, Muhammad Abid Nisar, in line with the professional standards required of a licensed Canadian immigration consultant.

What does an immigration consultation cost?

A short initial eligibility screen is free. Paid consultations start at CAD 75 for a document review and go up to CAD 200 for a direct session with our President, and every fee is credited in full toward your service cost if you proceed. See our consultation page for the full breakdown.

How long does the process take?

Timelines vary by program, applicant profile, document readiness, and government processing. We explain the current route and likely stages during your assessment.

Can you help after a refusal?

Yes. We can review the refusal reasons, identify gaps, and explain whether a stronger reapplication, reconsideration, or alternate pathway is appropriate.

Do I need to visit an office in person?

No. Our Mississauga and Lahore teams support clients in person and remotely, depending on your location and preference.

Which destinations do you cover?

Canada is our leading focus, with support available across 11 global destinations and pathways selected around your long-term goals.
