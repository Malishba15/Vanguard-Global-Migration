Overview

Landing in a new country is its own kind of project, separate from the immigration process itself. The Mississauga Landing Package is our practical, on-the-ground support for the first weeks after you arrive, delivered by a team based locally in Mississauga rather than a generic checklist.

What is included

  • A pre-arrival orientation call covering what to expect in your first week
  • Guidance through Social Insurance Number, provincial health card and bank account setup
  • A local housing orientation across Mississauga and the wider GTA, matched to your budget and commute needs
  • School registration guidance for families with school-age children
  • Structured check-ins through your first 90 days

How Vanguard helps

  • A pre-arrival checklist prepared before you land
  • In-person and remote support from our Mississauga office
  • Introductions to housing, banking and school resources relevant to your case
  • Ongoing check-ins through your first 90 days in Canada

Questions about Mississauga Landing Package

Is this only for permanent residents?+

No, it is available to study permit holders, work permit holders and new permanent residents alike.

Can this be added after my immigration application is already approved?+

Yes, many clients add this package once their approval is confirmed and a landing date is set.

Do you help with school enrollment for my children?+

Yes, we provide guidance on the school registration process for the Mississauga area.
