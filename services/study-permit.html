Overview

A study permit application is only as strong as the admission behind it. We start by targeting programs that are realistic for your academic profile and budget, not simply any acceptance letter, because a mismatched program is one of the most common reasons study permits are refused.

Under the Ministerial Instructions in effect for 2026, master’s and doctoral students admitted to a public designated learning institution are exempt from the Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL) requirement. Most college, undergraduate, and non-degree applicants still need a PAL from the province where they intend to study, issued before the study permit application.

Bringing your spouse

Spousal open work permit eligibility has been tightened as part of the broader 2025 and 2026 international student reforms, and it now depends on your specific program level and study permit status rather than applying uniformly to every student. We confirm your family’s current options as part of your file, since these rules have changed more than once in this cycle.

Frequently Asked

Questions about Study in Canada

Can my spouse work in Canada while I study?+

This depends on your program level and current study permit rules, which we confirm case by case given recent changes.

Do I need a PAL for a Master’s program?+

Not if you are admitted to a public designated learning institution; the PAL exemption currently applies to master’s and doctoral students at public DLIs.

How long does a study permit take to process?+

Processing times vary by country and season; we give you a current estimate at your consultation.
