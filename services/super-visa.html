Overview

IRCC paused new Parents and Grandparents Program (PGP) sponsorship intake in mid-2026, with existing applications still being processed but no new interest-to-sponsor invitations issued until further notice. The Super Visa remains fully open as the practical alternative: a multiple-entry visa valid for up to 10 years, allowing stays of up to 5 years per entry, far beyond a standard visitor visa.

The income and insurance requirements were also relaxed in the most recent update: sponsors can now show 75% of the required income in the most recent year, topped up by the visiting parent or grandparent’s own income, rather than needing to meet 100% of the threshold alone.

What you need to qualify

  • Proof of the parent-child or grandparent-grandchild relationship
  • Sponsor income meeting the relaxed threshold, topped up by the visitor’s income where needed
  • Canadian medical insurance valid for at least 1 year, from an approved insurer
  • A clean medical exam result

Frequently Asked

Questions about Super Visa

Is PGP sponsorship completely closed?+

New intake is paused, not permanently closed. Existing applications continue processing, and we watch for the next intake window.

Can my parent stay the full 10 years on one visa?+

The visa itself can be valid up to 10 years, but each individual stay is capped at up to 5 years before a further extension or re-entry is needed.

What changed with the income rules?+

Sponsors can now combine 75% of the required income with the visiting parent or grandparent’s own income, rather than meeting the full threshold from the sponsor’s income alone.
