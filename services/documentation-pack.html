Overview

Most application delays are not caused by eligibility problems, they are caused by paperwork: a missing employer letter, an outdated Educational Credential Assessment, or a police certificate that was never requested in time. The Documentation Pack exists to remove that risk entirely.

What we manage

  • Experience letters formatted to the specific standard IRCC and provincial bodies expect, coordinated directly with your current or former employers.
  • Educational Credential Assessment (ECA) through a designated body such as WES or IQAS, matched to the program you are applying under.
  • Certified translations for any document not already in English or French.
  • Police certificates from every country you have lived in for 6 months or more since turning 18, sourced through the correct authority for each country.

How Vanguard helps

  • A document checklist tailored specifically to your application
  • Direct coordination with employers and issuing authorities on your behalf
  • Vendor management for ECA and certified translation providers
  • A final quality check against IRCC requirements before submission

Questions about Documentation Pack

Which ECA body should I use?+

This depends on the program you are applying under; we confirm the correct designated organization for your case.

How far in advance should I request police certificates?+

As early as possible, some countries take weeks or months to issue them; we build this into your overall timeline.

Can you request documents from a country I no longer have contacts in?+

In most cases yes, we know the correct process and issuing authority for each country we work with regularly.
