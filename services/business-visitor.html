Overview

Business visitors use the same Temporary Resident Visa as regular visitors, declaring a business purpose rather than tourism. No work permit is required, as long as your primary source of income and your main place of business remain outside Canada. Typical qualifying activities include meetings, negotiations, conferences, trade shows, after-sales service, and site visits.

Where this crosses into needing a work permit

The line between a business visitor and someone who needs a work permit comes down to whether you are entering the Canadian labour market. Hands-on delivery of services performed for a Canadian client, rather than observing, negotiating, or attending, usually requires a work permit instead. We confirm which side of that line your trip falls on before you apply.

Frequently Asked

Questions about Business Visitor

Do I need a work permit to attend a trade show?+

Generally no, attending, exhibiting, or negotiating at a trade show is a recognized business visitor activity.

Can our whole delegation apply together?+

Yes, we prepare coordinated applications for delegations travelling and attending together.

What if my trip also involves hands-on work?+

That likely requires a work permit rather than business visitor status; we confirm this before you travel.
