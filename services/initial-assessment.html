Overview

We do not believe a substantive consultation should be free, because that usually means it is run by a salesperson rather than a licensed consultant. What we do offer for free is a short eligibility screen, by form or a brief call, to confirm that a paid consultation is actually the right next step for your case before you spend anything.

From there, you choose the depth of session you need: a document review, a live strategy consultation, or a session directly with our President, Muhammad Abid Nisar, RCIC R416527, for complex or previously refused cases.
