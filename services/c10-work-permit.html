C10 Work Permit: significant benefit permits for exceptional cases

Like C11, the C10 work permit is LMIA-exempt under the same significant-benefit provision, but it is typically used for intra-company transfers and cases tied to an existing foreign parent or affiliate company, not independent founders.

Overview

C10 shares its legal basis with C11, IRPR section 205(a), but the typical applicant looks very different. C10 is most often used by employees of multinational companies transferring into a Canadian affiliate, or by applicants whose case brings a genuinely exceptional social, cultural, or economic benefit that does not fit neatly into the intra-company transfer or owner-operator categories.

Because it does not follow a standard template the way C11 or an intra-company transfer does, C10 applications require a strong, individually argued case, and IRCC scrutinizes them closely.

Is C10 the right category for you?

Most applicants who ask about C10 actually fit a faster, better-defined category, whether that is an intra-company transfer, C11, or a standard LMIA-backed work permit. We tell you plainly if that is the case rather than filing an application that is unlikely to succeed.

If you work for a multinational with a Canadian affiliate, an intra-company transfer category may fit better; we assess this directly.
