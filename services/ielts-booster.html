Overview

CLB 10 is a high proficiency benchmark, roughly equivalent to an IELTS score of 8.0 or higher across all four bands. Reaching it can add significant CRS points on its own and strengthens eligibility for several category draws. Our coaching partner runs a structured program built around this specific target, not general English tuition.

How the program runs

Candidates start with a diagnostic test to identify their current band scores, then move through targeted modules in reading, writing, listening and speaking, with regular mock exams under timed conditions. Progress is tracked against your CRS strategy, so coaching stops when you have what you need, not on a fixed generic schedule.

How long does reaching CLB 10 usually take?+

Most serious candidates see meaningful progress in 6 to 10 weeks of intensive coaching, though this varies by starting level.

Is this only for Express Entry?+

No, a stronger language score can also help with several PNP streams and certain work permit categories.

What if I plateau below CLB 10?+

We reassess honestly and look at whether a different pathway, such as a category draw with a lower language bar, fits your profile better.
