Overview

The French-language proficiency category invites Express Entry candidates who score NCLC 7 or higher on a recognized French test, regardless of their English level. Through 2026, this category has run the largest draws by volume and consistently cleared at CRS scores well below general draws, which makes it one of the most reliable ways to move a stalled profile forward.

Why French changes your CRS math

Strong French ability adds CRS points on its own, and it separately qualifies you for category-based selection, which is scored against a much smaller, targeted pool rather than every candidate in Express Entry. For candidates within reach of NCLC 7, this is often the fastest lever available to change an outcome.

Our coaching partnership

We work with a coaching partner to deliver structured TEF Canada or TCF Canada preparation: a diagnostic assessment, targeted modules across reading, writing, listening and speaking, regular mock exams, and progress check-ins tied directly to your CRS strategy, not generic language learning.
